#1 - FLCL

FLCL is my all time favourite anime!! The animation is incredible as well as the metaphorical coming of age. It shows you how you shouldn't take things too seriously and embrace your weirdness. Although I don't think I'll ever rewatch it in fear of disliking it my second watch and whatnot.

#2 - Look Back

Look back is an amazing movie about how it feels to be an artist, or any sort of creator really! It's super relatable in the sense that it touches on jealousy, talent, and how it feels to want to become skilled at something. I also quite like the manga though the movie made me appreciate it more!

#3 - False Memory

Just an amazing show with crazy animation!!
